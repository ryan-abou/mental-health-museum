# Turning Outward

A virtual museum of the right to mental health, made for AP Lang and Comp (Dr. Gingrich, November 2026).

**Visit:** https://ryan-abou.github.io/mental-health-museum/

The museum has a lobby and five galleries, each with two artifacts, plus an exit room with resources:

| Room | Curator |
| --- | --- |
| 1. Mental Health Stigma | Hanrui Huang |
| 2. Mental Health on Social Media | Ryan Abourjeili |
| 3. Mental Health in the Workplace | Nithil Arun |
| 4. Mental Health at School | Jewel McCallum |
| 5. *Turtles All the Way Down* (book room) | The whole team |

## Editing

All of the museum's text is in `content.js`. To add the finished PSA video, paste its YouTube link into `PSA_VIDEO_URL` at the top of that file.

The artifact drawings are in `art.js`, and the layout is in `app.js` and `styles.css`.

If you or someone you know is struggling, call or text 988.
